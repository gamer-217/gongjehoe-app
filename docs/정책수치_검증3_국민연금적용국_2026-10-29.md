## Clearly excluded for locally hired E-9 / H-2 workers (no NPS contributions at the workplace)
By reciprocity (사업장·지역 모두 적용제외), all nationalities and visas:
- Nepal, Myanmar, Bangladesh, Pakistan, Timor-Leste (E-9)
- Kazakhstan, Belarus (H-2)

By the social security agreement, E-9 only:
- Uzbekistan: E-9 holders are exempt without submitting a certificate (NPS 2025.2 table note).
- Mongolia: likely exempt. The NPS foreigner guide says sending workers (송출근로자) from Mongolia and Uzbekistan follow home-country law, but the current table excerpt has no explicit E-9 note. Treat as PARTIAL.

NOT excluded at the workplace (사업장 당연적용):
- Vietnam, Indonesia, Cambodia, Sri Lanka, Thailand, Laos, Mongolia (by reciprocity, apart from the agreement point above), Philippines, Kyrgyzstan, China, Russia, Ukraine, Azerbaijan.
- China H-2 workers are locally hired. The China agreement covers only dispatched workers, so they pay NPS.

Unresolved:
- Tajikistan and Turkmenistan (absent from the excerpts; the default rule makes them 당연적용 if they are not listed).
- Uzbek H-2 holders: the E-9 note doesn't cover them, and the country's reciprocity class was not found.
- The full 2026.3.19 list of 20 excluded countries: whether Nepal, Myanmar, Bangladesh and Timor-Leste are still on it is only implied, by 2026 secondary summaries and the count dropping 21 to 20, which matches Malaysia moving out.
