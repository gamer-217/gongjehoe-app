## Notes for the design doc
1. **Vietnam:** treat as covered at 사업장 from 2022-01-01. The older "excluded" guide is out of date.
2. **E-8:** do not group seasonal E-8 workers with E-9/H-2 for 반환일시금. NPS says the current E-8 is not eligible. E-8 can opt out of 장기요양 since the May 2026 amendment, which still needs checking.
3. **2.2%:** not an official number. The law uses the 3-year term-deposit rate for each period. The app should label any rate it uses as an estimate.
4. **Indonesia 지역 status** and the **content of 법률 제21203호 (제126조 amendment, 2025.12.16)** need the documents opened before release.
